Privacy Statement

Last updated 2 July 2026.

RiverLaunch.app ("RiverLaunch", "we", "us") respects your privacy. This statement explains what personal data we collect, how we use it, and the rights you have. It covers the RiverLaunch app and website.

Who we are

RiverLaunch.app is a community river-intelligence app for paddlers. For any privacy question or request, contact support@riverlaunch.app.

What we collect

  • Account details — when you create an account: your email address and display name (handled through Firebase Authentication, including Google sign-in if you choose it).
  • Content you add — contributions (access points, hazards, features, reports), photos, paddle logs, and club and meetup activity, plus any text you enter.
  • Location — the map centres on places you look at; if you turn on live location, your device location is used to show where you are. We do not track your location in the background.
  • Usage & device data — with your consent, we collect anonymous analytics (which features are used) to improve the app. Basic technical data is processed to run the service securely.

How we use your data

  • To provide and operate the app — accounts, maps, contributions, clubs, and meetups.
  • To moderate community contributions and keep safety-relevant information honest.
  • To improve the app, using analytics where you have consented.
  • To respond to your support requests.

Legal bases (UK GDPR)

  • Contract — to provide the service you sign up for.
  • Consent — analytics, and optional features you switch on (such as a public profile or live location).
  • Legitimate interests — keeping the service secure, moderating content, and improving the app.

Sharing

We use trusted service providers to run RiverLaunch — notably Google Firebase for authentication, database, storage, hosting, and analytics. We do not sell your personal data. Content you choose to make public — such as approved contributions, a public paddler profile, or photos — is visible to others.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. Email support@riverlaunch.app. You can withdraw analytics consent at any time. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Keeping your data

We keep your account and contributions while your account is active. Approved community contributions may remain part of the shared dataset after your account closes, but are separated from your personal identity where practical.

Children

RiverLaunch is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.

Changes

We may update this statement; the "last updated" date above shows when. We will highlight significant changes in the app.
